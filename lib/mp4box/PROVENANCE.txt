mp4box.js — ISOBMFF (MP4) parser/demuxer in JavaScript.

Version:  0.5.2  (npm dist/mp4box.all.min.js, unmodified)
Source:   https://www.npmjs.com/package/mp4box  /  https://github.com/gpac/mp4box.js
Fetched:  2026-09-05
License:  BSD-3-Clause (LICENSE alongside)

Why here: loading/video.js demuxes MP4 samples (offsets, sizes, cts, sync
          flags, avcC/hvcC description) and feeds them to WebCodecs
          VideoDecoder. Loaded as a classic <script> (defines global MP4Box and
          DataStream), and via importScripts() in workers. Same lineage as
          luc3d's loading/video.js; luc3d has since moved its default decode
          backend to mediabunny (via sleap-io.js) for B-frame PTS correctness —
          calibrat3 instead sorts samples by cts (presentation order) and
          decodes whole GOPs, which handles B-frames for our batch use case.

Re-fetch: npm pack mp4box@<ver>; copy dist/mp4box.all.min.js.
